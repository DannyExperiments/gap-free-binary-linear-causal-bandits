# Status

[Version 1.0.0](https://github.com/DannyExperiments/gap-free-binary-linear-causal-bandits/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · 27 November 2026 · [Version DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24571499)

The main theorem gives a gap-free statistical regret bound for the fully observed binary-linear Markovian model, polynomial in node count and square-root in the horizon up to a logarithm. A credited companion argument gives the minimax order for the atomic-only menu with at least two intervenable nodes.

The argument has undergone internal mathematical review. External peer review and formal verification are pending. The [scope](CLAIM_SCOPE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md) records the computational and modeling limits.
