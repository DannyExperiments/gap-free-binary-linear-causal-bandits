# Mathematical audit

The rounded true model remains acyclic and row-substochastic. Topological propagation bounds the largest coordinate error by d times the largest row discretization error, uniformly over interventions. Loss of this rounded model differs from the loss of the true mean by at most two over the full horizon.

The predictable action makes the inner product of the current mean discrepancy with centered observations conditionally centered. The entire inner product has a bounded range. Hoeffding's lemma and a finite model/prefix union bound establish simultaneous confidence without coordinate independence. This gives a past squared-discrepancy budget even for a different selected model in each round.

On rounds when a row is free, the difference in its linear mean equation gives a residual, bounded in terms of the full mean-vector discrepancy. The determinant potential's design uses true means only in the proof. Propagating residuals in the candidate's acyclic order bounds reward error, and the sum of rowwise widths yields square-root horizon dependence. The algorithm itself uses only rational predictions and observed losses.

For atomic actions, checked the common strictly positive baseline, the gap against all competing signed actions, conditional KL via a Bernoulli chi-square bound, and the Pinsker constant through small and large horizons. The public proof directly adapts the rare-root family and KL mechanism of Lattimore, Lattimore and Reid. MOSS supplies the matching upper bound for this menu.
